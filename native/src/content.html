Dog Breed Quiz: Which Dog Is Right for You?

Find a starting point for your next best friendship. Answer 12 questions about your home, routine, and preferences to discover three breed matches, then explore adoption or puppy pages for your state.

How does the dog breed quiz work?

The quiz compares your answers with nine breed profiles and ranks three possible matches. It considers living space, children and other pets, dog-owning experience, exercise, time together, training, grooming, personality, barking tolerance, and independence. Your adoption or puppy preference and state determine where your result links lead.

Your result also includes a playful dog-parent personality card. This is a fun summary of your preferences, not a scientific personality assessment.

What should you consider when choosing a dog?

Your daily routine

Think about the exercise, companionship, training, and grooming you can consistently provide—not just what a perfect weekend might look like. Your available time matters as much as your home's size.

Your household and preferences

Consider everyone who shares your home, including children and other animals. Your comfort with barking, an energetic playmate, or a dog who prefers close company can help guide your search.

The individual dog

A breed match is a starting point, not a guarantee. Age, health, temperament, history, socialization, and training all matter. Meet the individual dog and discuss their needs with the rescue, shelter, or breeder before deciding.

Explore adoption and puppy options

Browse dogs for adoption or puppies for sale. Quiz results link to breed-and-state pages; the quiz does not check live inventory or guarantee a dog is available.

Dog breed quiz questions, answered

How long does the quiz take?

There are 12 questions. You can move back to review answers and retake the quiz to explore different preferences.

Does it include every breed?

No. This version compares nine breed profiles. A dog outside that selection, including a mixed-breed dog, could be a good fit for you too.

Will it tell me which dogs are available near me?

It directs you to state-specific breed pages for your chosen adoption or puppy route. Check those pages and contact the provider to confirm current availability and location.

Can I share my results?

Yes. Use the result-sharing buttons or copy a result link. That link includes your quiz selections, including your chosen state, so share it only if you are comfortable making those preferences visible.

Is this professional advice?

No. This is general breed-selection guidance, not a veterinary or behavioral assessment. Discuss any specific health, behavior, or household concerns with an appropriate professional.
